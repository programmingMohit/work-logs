### Investigation & Research

#### lab-blr Redmi proxy outage and the router on the lab board
- **Repo:** `handoffs`
- **Question:** The proxy behind the Redmi's USB tunnel in lab-blr stopped answering at about 10:13 UTC. Is a TP-Link router on the lab board, and is it asking for an IP?
- **Outcome:** The router was sending DHCP requests, but nothing on the board answered them. Wrote a single root script, `scripts/lab/pulse-dhcp.sh`, that runs a DHCP server on the board. From its MAC, the router turned out to be a different unit swapped in around 16:26 IST: the new one starts `98:03:8e`, the old one `58:d8:12`. It's a spare TL-WR802N v4, not the Pulse test unit. Wrote the handoff as a new doc in the KB docs collection and fixed it after the wrong Pulse-firmware claim was pointed out.
- **Time:** 16:00–16:59

#### Spare TL-WR802N plugged into the Mac over USB
- **Repo:** `Nimbly` (workspace)
- **Question:** What is on the Realtek RTL8153 USB Ethernet link, and can we tell which software the TL-WR802N is running?
- **Outcome:** The link is up at 100 Mb, but the router doesn't answer ping or management requests. It doesn't answer ping on its Ethernet port, which OpenWrt does by default, so it probably isn't running OpenWrt. Turned on Internet Sharing so it could get an address. The next step is to listen to its DNS and NTP lookups while it restarts to identify the software. Saved a summary of what's visible at `/tmp/wr802n-view.html`.
- **Time:** 15:40–16:19
