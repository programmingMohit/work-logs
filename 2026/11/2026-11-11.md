- **Repo:** `audit-lite`
- **Question:** Do the milestone 51 QA scenarios pass for the v2.18.0 OTA, is Notion issue 2219.1 (module cards stay after an offline cold start on Android) worth a dev look, and why did the Maestro suite fail on a fresh build?
- **Outcome:** Maestro showed that on a brand-new install the app never starts uploading photos. This is likely a real bug and probably a release blocker. #### Device lab: Android battery drain on the Orange Pi USB hub
- **Repo:** `discovery`
- **Question:** Why were the Android phones in lab-blr draining battery during about 12 hours of continuous test runs, and can the hub fix it?
- **Outcome:** The batteries drained steadily from about 22:15 IST. The hub port supplies only about 0.5 A, like a plain data port, which isn't enough to keep the Galaxy charged under load. Changing ports or software settings doesn't raise the limit. The fix is a charging-capable port or power delivery, not a bigger hub. Findings are in `discovery-device-lab-orange-pi-hardware.md`. Testlab reservation #57 was ended at 12:19 IST.
