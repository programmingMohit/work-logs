#### Stuck reports on dashboard 28 / FBI iOS case (Fizzy 1776)
- **Repo:** `audit-lite`
- **Question:** Are reports still getting stuck after the outbox fixes, which org is worst hit, and why is a sample FBI report stuck?
- **Outcome:** Reports are still getting stuck, and FBI is the worst-affected org. A sample FBI report was traced through PostHog, GCP and Cloudflare logs. On Nov 14, 19 `update-answers` PATCHes (14:32–15:19) came from iOS 27.2 build 40, device uuid `8F1055F9-EAD6-4D25-8795-1BC7703322ED`. A signed-URL POST for report `2026-11-14_1794666775736` followed at 16:18:23 on OTA `01a1cd5b`. The only other device on that login is an Android. The investigation also measured how long after submit the app was backgrounded and how long the user stayed on the sending screen; those answers are in the session, not in this log. The cause is iOS uploads that depend on the app staying in the foreground. Write-up: `docs/analysis/fbi-stuck-report-ios-foreground-uploads-2026-11-16.md`.
