#### Redesign of the Pulse Step 3 calibration walk (mockups)
- **Repo:** `Nimbly` (scratchpad HTML, not committed)
- **Question:** How should the new Step 3 walk look for calibrators? The new flow is two parts: a walk along the inner boundary of the store, then coverage of the floor.
- **Outcome:** Built interactive HTML mockups (`pulse-walk-v1.html`, `step3-islands.html`) and hosted them on the dev server through `/nimbly:deploy-report-public`. Decisions:
  - The walk starts just inside the store, with no outside-door segment like today's spot 1.
  - Progress is a percentage of the minimum required readings, not a fixed walk time. "Keep walking" counts down the percent left and shows no raw numbers.
  - "Walk slowly" moved from a banner to a bullet point in the instructions.
  - Part 2 asks the calibrator to cover as much of the floor as possible, not to walk in lanes. Its animation is a cross-hatch.
  - "Finish anyway" appears once 50% is done. There is also a hidden hold-to-finish that only support can unlock when a calibrator escalates.
  - Fixed the skinny "Start part 2" button in figure 3.
  - Terminology: "inline store" (enclosed mall unit) vs "island store"/kiosk (freestanding, no walls). Anchor stores count as inline for the walk.
