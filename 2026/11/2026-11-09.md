### Investigation & Research

#### Rolling back an Echo Vision OTA pushed to 100% of prod
- **Repo:** `ai` (echo-vision-app)
- **Question:** An OTA update was pushed by mistake to 100% of production users. How do we roll it back?
- **Outcome:** Wrote the steps up as a Notion runbook: [Echo Vision app: roll back a production OTA](https://app.notion.com/p/413c0014876981cfac52d1244ebc2952) (Type `Runbook`, Status `Living`, Area `mobile`). It covers:
  - finding the bad update group and the last good one with `eas channel:view` and `eas update:list`
  - a warning that an OTA ships everything on its ref, not just the commit in its message
  - checking the rollout percentage with `eas update:view` to decide between a full and an incremental rollback
  - the rollback steps for each case
- **Time:** 12:10–12:16
