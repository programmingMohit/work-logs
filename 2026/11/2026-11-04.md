#### Pulse calibration walk flow and PR #1134
- **Repo:** `ai`
- **Question:** What happens end to end in a calibration walk, from login to starting calibration, installing and connecting to the Pulse router, and on through the walk? Was PR #1134 needed if users can never reach the "start calibration" section, and would it break builds that have the camera and Wi-Fi libraries installed? Do the Pulse docs say who is supposed to do the calibration walks? Did Andi push to the `ai` repo yesterday?
- **Outcome:** Mapped the walk mechanics. Phones with Wi-Fi on send probe requests every 30 s to a few minutes, even when they never connect. The router overhears these and records a hashed device ID, the signal strength (RSSI) and a timestamp. Calls from the same phone are grouped into device sessions. Also reviewed PR #1134 to judge whether it is needed and whether it is safe on builds with the camera and Wi-Fi libraries. The same session produced PR #1135, which is listed above.
- **Time:** 08:57–15:00

#### Single-walk ML calibration and inside/outside classification limits
- **Repo:** `ai`
- **Question:** What is it called when one store's single walk is used to calibrate a model? What does Pulse calibration measure today? Is there any ML/AI plan in the docs, and are walks being stored as a labelled dataset? What is the v1 goal? With spot 5 (deepest inside the store) always reading weaker than spot 1 (outside), how can a phone be classified as inside?
- **Outcome:** With one router and only signal strength, Pulse measures distance from the router, not which side of the wall a phone is on. Its "inside" is a circle of signal strength around the router. That circle matches the store only if every inside spot is closer to the router, radio-wise, than the street. A walk where spot 5 is weaker than spot 1 breaks that assumption, and `walk.lua` is built to reject the placement rather than guess (`pulse/hardware/tplink-mr3020/firmware/files/usr/lib/pulse-calibration/walk.lua:58`).
- **Time:** 09:09–09:30
