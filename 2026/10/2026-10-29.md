#### Sushi Yay report data loss (Fizzy card 1776)
- **Repo:** `analysis` (`docs/analysis/`)
- **Question:** Was the Sushi Yay report loss reported on Discord caused by the outbox, and what actually happened to the report?
- **Outcome:**
  - **Not the outbox:** no submit attempt was ever made on the report.
  - **Root cause:** a draft was wiped through the "Continue" card path. `report.app.usecase.ts:1495` checks only `!reportMongo` and `status === 'complete'` before the hard `deleteOne` at `report.mongo.repository.ts:463`.
  - **Impact:** eight auditors are affected, not two. The data is recoverable until 2026-11-04.
  - **Verification:** HelloClaw independently verified the root cause, and the thread was asked to reprioritize the fixes.
  - **Docs written:** `sushiyay-continue-card-draft-wipe-2026-10-29.md` and `sushiyay-report-recovery-runbook-2026-10-29.md`.
