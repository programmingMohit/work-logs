### Investigation & Research

#### Duplicate check-in on a completed schedule slot
- **Repo:** `Nimbly` (api-schedules)
- **Question:** On Sep 17, how did auditor astariniagara end up with a second check-in, and a second report, on a schedule slot (19:30–21:30) that was already complete? And how could one account be signed in on two phones at once?
- **Outcome:** The account was signed in on more than one phone. At 21:25 the auditor tapped **Check in** on Phone B for the already-completed slot, and the server accepted it because nothing stops a check-in on a slot that already has a completed report. I wrote a CS-ready summary. The triage thread listed six fixes in two groups. The main one is a server-side check-in guard in api-schedules that refuses the check-in or returns the existing report, which fixes the problem however many phones are signed in. The bug was filed as P2 (due within 5 working days), moved to a new Discord bug thread, and is tracked on Fizzy card #2795.
