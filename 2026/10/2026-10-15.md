#### Relay vs smart plug for the test-phone charging hub
- **Repo:** `Nimbly`
- **Question:** What hardware is needed to switch a test phone's charging from the board's GPIO, and would a smart plug be cheaper or simpler?
- **Outcome:** Chose a relay module (about ₹930 including reusable tools) over a smart plug (about ₹700–1,000). The relay can switch only the Redmi while the Galaxy keeps its 80% cap, and it needs no Wi-Fi, which matters because BHIVE's captive portal would block a smart plug. Parts, a wire stripper and a screwdriver were added to the Amazon cart for same-day or next-day delivery and checked against the wiring explainer.
- **Time:** 00:07–09:33
