#### Orange Pi device lab: SD card check, Armbian flash and node setup
- **Repo:** `discovery`
- **Question:** Is the 64 GB SD card in the reader real capacity or fake? With no `f3probe` on macOS, how do we check it without bricking the card? After that, how do we flash Armbian and set the board up as a devlab node?
- **Outcome:**
  - Wrote a custom probe script (`sdprobe.py`) to test the card in place of f3, then flashed Armbian with `xz -dc … | dd of=/dev/rdisk4`.
  - Built the node provisioning:
    - Armbian preset script
    - `devlab-beacon` service and unit, plus a `listen-beacon.py` listener
    - `sync-devlab-users.sh` for user sync
    - A custom MOTD (`05-devlab`) showing a devlab-blr banner and lab, network, system and commands sections
  - Turned off the default Armbian MOTD with `MOTD_DISABLE` in `/etc/default/armbian-motd`, so package updates shouldn't bring it back. The original is backed up as `armbian-motd.orig`, and only the "reboot required" warning is kept. The WAN IP is cached and refreshed in the background every hour, so logins never wait on the network.
  - Checked the new login screen by logging in as `mohit`.
