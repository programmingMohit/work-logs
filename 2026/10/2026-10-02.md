### Investigation & Research

#### Choosing a powered USB hub for the device lab
- **Repo:** `discovery`
- **Question:** Which powered USB hub fits the device lab's needs (as specified in the Notion doc), is sold in India, and can have its ports controlled by `uhubctl`? Candidates were a powered hub on Amazon, hubs from `uhubctl`'s compatibility list, and the TP-Link UH700. A smart plug was also looked at as another way to control power. The session also checked on a Redmi test device that had been left running for about 12 hours.
- **Outcome:** Recommended the **TP-Link UH700** over the QZ Active Powered USB Hub:
  - **UH700:** ₹2,698, 7 data ports, 24W adapter included, 1.5A per port, so about 3 phones can charge at full speed at once. Rated 4.5★ from 6.2K reviews, with TP-Link service in India.
  - **QZ Active:** ₹2,049, 4 ports, 2.4A per port, so only 2 phones at full speed. Rated 4.4★ from 1.3K reviews, from a lesser-known brand.
  - **Why the UH700:** the lab phones stay plugged in and topped up all day, so the QZ's faster charging matters little. The extra ports and brand support matter more.

  The draft research doc (scratchpad `s15.md`) was updated with these findings.
