`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- The RCR fix (`a0f31bdf`) is a classic MongoDB aggregation gotcha: an unused `$addFields` stage still forces the server to materialize/scan documents through the pipeline, and on a large collection that can blow past the driver's default socket timeout even though the stage's output is never read. Removing dead stages is a real perf fix, not just cleanup.
- The `nimblee` sequence is a textbook resilience arc — the two `feat:` commits deliver the happy path, then the three `fix:` commits each close a distinct failure mode (partial-failure rollback, transient-error cache poisoning, stale reservations). That ordering is the actual story of the branch.
