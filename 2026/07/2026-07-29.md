`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- The commit ordering here tells a story worth noting: `dae921f8` fixing "define the background task in the app entry file" *after* the PR was opened is the classic Expo background-task gotcha — `TaskManager.defineTask()` must run at module scope during app boot, not inside a component or route file, or the OS wakes the app and finds no registered handler.
- The `refactor(prefetch): extract route-independent prefetch params` commit sitting before the actual prefetch feature is good sequencing: background jobs have no navigation context, so any param derived from the current route has to be lifted out first.
