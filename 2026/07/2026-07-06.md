`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- The `dayjs.tz()` crash is a classic offline gotcha: `dayjs.tz()` leans on the browser's `Intl` timezone database, which can be unavailable or throw in constrained/offline runtimes — hence the defensive guard rather than assuming `Intl` is always present.
