#### Check-in button visibility probe
- **Repo:** `Nimbly-Technologies/audit-lite` · branch: `chore/checkin-button-visibility-probe`
- **What was done:** Added telemetry (no behaviour change) to diagnose a customer report of a vanished check-in button on an OPPO A60 (ColorOS) that blocked the checklist. Ruled out the reported large-system-font cause through device comparison — it would take ~3.8x scale to push the button off screen, and the customer's screenshot proved the button was rendered but invisible. Rather than probing the leading ColorOS zero-inset theory (which would return misleading silence if wrong), the probe measures the actual outcome: whether the button's rect lands inside the usable viewport, carrying enough geometry to derive the cause afterwards.
