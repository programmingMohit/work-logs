- Android 13+ split the old `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` into granular `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`. Play now requires a declared-purpose form for broad media access — most apps avoid it entirely by using the Photo Picker (`ACTION_PICK_IMAGES`), which needs zero permissions.
