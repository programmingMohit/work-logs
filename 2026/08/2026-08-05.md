`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- The password-policy work is a textbook **schema-first dependency chain**: the field has to exist on `OrganizationSchema` (entity-node) and be released before `api-organizations` or `audit-admin` can meaningfully read/write it — hence entity-node merging first at 05:06 and the release at 05:07, then api-organizations at 06:45 and audit-admin at 06:29.
- Collapsing two `validatePassword` copies into a shared module is what makes the Mongo-backed config actually meaningful — a per-service copy would mean org policy silently applies in some flows and not others (single-user vs. bulk creation being exactly that gap).
- The gallery-V2 and gallery-count fix follows the standard **dual-read behind a flag** pattern: the flag decides the read source, so counts and contents can't diverge for orgs mid-migration.
