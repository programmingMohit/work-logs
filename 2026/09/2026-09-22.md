#### Submission screen fixes and a dedicated rejection screen
- **Repo:** `audit-lite` · branch: `fix/submission-cancelled-request-terminal`
- **What was done:**
  - **Diagnosis:** A flight-recorder log from a OnePlus CPH2487 on Android 16 showed "0 file(s) still need sending" while 19 attachments were still queued, and the retake/retry sections never appeared.
